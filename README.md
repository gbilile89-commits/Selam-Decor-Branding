# Selam-Decor-Branding & Digital Marketing
Brand identity, graphic design, and social media content developed for Selam Decor.

## Project Overview

Selam Decor is a visual branding and digital marketing project focused on building an elegant and consistent online presence for a wedding decoration and floral brand.

## My Role

- Graphic Designer
- Digital Marketer
- Brand Identity Development
- Social Media Content Design

## Project Objectives

- Develop a recognizable visual identity
- Create an elegant and consistent brand style
- Design engaging social media content
- Strengthen the brand's online presence

## Branding

### Logo
The visual identity was developed around an elegant and sophisticated aesthetic suitable for wedding decoration and floral services.

### Color Palette

- Dust Green
- Cream / White
- Gold

### Typography

Elegant script typography was combined with complementary supporting fonts to create a refined and memorable visual identity.

## Social Media Design

The project included the development of:

- Instagram posts
- Promotional graphics
- Wedding-related content
- Social media campaign visuals
- Promotional video concepts

## Tools

- Adobe Photoshop
- Adobe Illustrator

## Skills Demonstrated

**Graphic Design:**  
Brand identity, logo design, typography, color selection, social media design, visual composition.

**Digital Marketing:**  
Social media content planning, promotional campaigns, audience-focused messaging, and brand communication.

## Project Outcome

The project established a cohesive visual direction for Selam Decor and created a foundation for consistent digital communication across social media platforms.
